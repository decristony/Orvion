Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently
Asked Questions

Have questions? Our FAQ section has you covered with quick answers to the most common inquiries.

What do I need to get started?

Nothing technical. Just your goals and ideas. We take it from there — our team handles research, strategy, design, and launch with you involved at every milestone.

What kind of customization is available?

Everything is fully customizable: colors, fonts, layouts, sections, and content. Every element can be edited to match your brand's identity and needs.

How easy is it to edit for beginners?

Very easy. The design is built with clean, simple controls — swap images, change text, and rearrange blocks without touching a single line of code.

Let me know more about moneyback guarantee?

Every purchase is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. If the template doesn't meet your expectations, we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Everything works visually. But if you do code, the clean structure makes it easy to extend and integrate with your existing stack.

What will I get after purchasing the template?

You get the full template package: all page sections, brand assets, source files, documentation, lifetime updates, and email support to help you launch.
