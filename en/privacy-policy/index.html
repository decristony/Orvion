Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ORVION collects and uses information when you browse the website, contact us or request a quote, and outlines rights related to personal data.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

01

What data we collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate the website and respond to enquiries. This may include name, email address, phone number and message content submitted through contact channels. We may also collect technical usage data such as IP address, browser, device, pages viewed, traffic source and interactions.

02

How we use data

Information may be used to respond to enquiries and quote requests, provide support, understand website usage, improve content and user experience, measure marketing performance and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

03

Legal basis

Where applicable, processing may rely on consent, ORVION's legitimate interest in measuring and improving the website, and steps related to a requested proposal or service engagement, subject to applicable data-protection law.

04

Cookies and tracking technologies

The website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand traffic and behaviour. We currently use tools such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. You can block or remove cookies through your browser settings, although some functionality may be affected.

05

Who we share data with

ORVION does not sell personal data. Information may be processed by providers required to operate the website and communications, including hosting, email, analytics and communication providers such as Google, Microsoft and Meta/WhatsApp where applicable.

06

How long we retain data

We retain information for as long as reasonably required for the purposes described above, while a commercial relationship or enquiry is active, and for any additional period required by applicable obligations. Data may then be deleted or anonymized.

07

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, alteration or misuse. No digital environment is completely risk-free, but we take the protection of information seriously.

08

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, request portability and obtain information about how your data is handled.

09

How to exercise your rights

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal data, contact contato@orvionweb.com. We may request limited additional information when reasonably necessary to verify the identity of the requester.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the website, our services or applicable requirements. The current version will be available at orvionweb.com/en/privacy-policy/, with the update date shown at the top.
